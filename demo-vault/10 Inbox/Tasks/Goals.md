# Goals

What the goals' tree on the Tasks page is read from. A goal is a task with
#goal; the steps written under it are its stages and tasks, and the ids after
⛔ tie in tasks from other notes — the shoes from the inbox, a unit from the
Arabic project. A step with a day shows up in the task list on that day. A
step with 🎯 is done by hours, not a tick: so many in all, or so many a week.
