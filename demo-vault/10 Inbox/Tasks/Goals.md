# Goals

What the goals' tree on the Tasks page is read from. A goal is a task with
#goal, and #goal/health files it under an area; the steps written under it
are its stages and tasks, and the ids after
⛔ tie in tasks from other notes — the shoes from the inbox, a unit from the
Arabic project. A step that is a link to a project is as far along as the
project's tasks. A step with a day shows up in the task list on that day. A
step with 🎯 is done by hours or by times, not a tick: so many in all, or so
many a week; 🔢 is how many times so far.
